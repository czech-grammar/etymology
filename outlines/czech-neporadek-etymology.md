- Czech nepořádek etymology
  - Meaning: “mess”
    - Mess
    - Disorder
  - Word history
    - From ne- + pořádek
    - Root: Proto-Slavic *porędъkъ
    - From *poręditi, “to line up” + *-ъkъ
    - From *po-, “after” + *ręditi, “to arrange”
  - Cousins of the root *porędъkъ
    - Polish porządek
    - Slovak poriadok
    - Russian порядок
